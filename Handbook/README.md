### Key Rules Summary

#### Team Requirements
- Minimum: 1 student + 1 faculty advisor
- Maximum QLabs access: 10 members
- Team captain must attend Stage 3 in New Orleans, LA

#### Virtual Stage (Stage 1)
- Develop in QLabs simulation
- Submit video demonstrating capabilities
- Deadline: February 27, 2026

#### Physical Stage (Stages 2 & 3)
- Top 6 teams advance
- Receive physical QCar 2
- Implement on real hardware
- Compete live at ACC conference

### Scoring Criteria

Video submissions will be evaluated on:

1. **Technical Capability**
   - Navigation performance
   - Traffic rule compliance
   - Obstacle handling

2. **Algorithm Sophistication**
   - Perception pipeline
   - Planning approach
   - Control implementation

3. **Presentation Quality**
   - Clear demonstration
   - System explanation
   - Team presentation
